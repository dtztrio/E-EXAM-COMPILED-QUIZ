General Overview

DTZ TRIO is a dedicated study and assessment platform designed to help students master course materials through practice quizzes, real-time timer simulations, and worked explanations.
Yes, our core tools, quizzes, and practice calculators are completely free for students to access anytime.

Practice & Timer Modes

Automatic Timer gives instant feedback after every question, ideal for learning. Custom Timer lets you configure strict time limits without pauses or instant answers, simulating real exam conditions.
Questions and multiple-choice options are randomized on every test attempt to ensure you are testing concept comprehension rather than memorizing position sequences.

Privacy & Settings

Your preferences, theme options, and practice history are stored locally on your device browser. No personal diagnostic data is transmitted or sold.
Go to the Settings page from the main menu bar. There, you can adjust theme preference (Dark/Light) and scale the interface font size.
