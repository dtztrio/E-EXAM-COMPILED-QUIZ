How each mode works
Automatic Timer runs all 100 questions with a clock the app manages for you. Tap an option and it's locked in immediately — correct turns green, wrong turns red, with a short explanation right away. Tap Pause any time to freeze the clock and blur the screen. You must answer at least 30 questions before you can submit.
Custom Timer lets you choose specific topics, your own time limit (up to 120 min), and how many questions to include. It cannot be paused — answers stay hidden until you submit, then you get a full score breakdown and review.
A full scientific calculator — sin/cos/tan, log, ln, √, powers, π and e — is available as a draggable floating window in both modes.
Your progress is auto-saved as you go, so a refresh won't lose your place. For fairness, switching away from the tab is tracked — leaving 3 times in one quiz resets it.
